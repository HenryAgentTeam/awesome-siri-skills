# Track assignments due

Use the List Assignments tool to get the user's assignments with their due dates and subjects. Sort them by due date, soonest first, and point out anything due today or tomorrow.

Tell the user the next few assignments in order, with the subject and the due date for each, and suggest which one to start with. Keep the list short and keep each item to one line.

## Don't

- Don't invent assignments or due dates the tools did not return.
- Don't mark anything as done unless the user says so.
- Don't share the user's schoolwork with anyone else.
