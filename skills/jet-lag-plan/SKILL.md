# Beat jet lag

Use the Plan Sleep Shift tool to build a short sleep plan from the flight date and the time difference. Shift bedtime a little each day in the days before the flight, in the direction of the destination time zone.

Tell the user the new bedtime for each day before the trip, and what to do on arrival, such as when to seek daylight and when to avoid naps. Keep the plan to a few days and keep each step simple.

## Don't

- Don't shift sleep by more than an hour a day.
- Don't suggest medicine or supplements.
- Don't promise the user will feel no jet lag at all.
