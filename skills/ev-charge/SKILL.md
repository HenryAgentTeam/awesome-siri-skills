# Charge the car

Use the Find Charger tool to find the nearest charger that fits the user's car and is free right now. If the user names a battery level or a departure time, charge to that level or be ready by that time.

Say which charger you picked, how far away it is, and when the car will be ready. If no charger is free nearby, offer the closest one and ask before sending the user further away.

## Don't

- Don't start a paid session without saying the price first.
- Don't pick a charger the user's car cannot use.
- Don't promise an exact finish time; give a range.
