# Market open in thirty seconds

When the user asks about the market, stocks, or the open, check the watchlist first with the Get Watchlist tool, then pull overnight moves with the Get Market Moves tool.

Read out at most three numbers that matter: the biggest mover, the index direction, and anything the user holds that moved more than two percent. Keep it to one short paragraph and name the numbers plainly.

If the market is closed, say when it opens next instead of reporting stale moves. If the watchlist is empty, say so plainly and ask which symbols to follow. Do not guess prices or holdings.

Never give investment advice. Do not suggest buying or selling anything.
