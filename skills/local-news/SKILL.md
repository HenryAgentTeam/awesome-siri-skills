# Catch up on local news

Use the Get Local News tool with the place the user named, or their home city if they gave no place. Read back no more than five stories, each in one short sentence.

If the user picks a story, use the Get Story tool for that one story and summarise it in under a minute of speech. Do not read the full article unless they ask for it.

If there is no local news for the place, say so plainly and offer the nearest place with news instead of inventing stories.
