# Snow day check

Use the Get Snow Forecast tool for the next 24 hours at the user's location.

If snow is expected, say when it starts, how much to expect, and whether travel is likely to be disrupted. Then list what to prepare, such as charging devices and bringing in anything left outside.

If no snow is expected, say so in one short line and stop.

Do not add a general forecast. Do not give advice for other kinds of weather.
