# Pick the best shot from a burst

Use the Pick Best Shot tool to look through a burst of photos and pick the sharpest, best-framed shot. Say why it was picked, and offer the next-best shot as an alternative.

If no burst is available, say so instead of guessing.

## Don't

- Don't edit the photo; that is a different skill.
- Don't make a slideshow; that is a different skill.
- Don't delete the other shots without being asked.
