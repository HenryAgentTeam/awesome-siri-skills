# Weekend weather outlook

Use the Get Forecast tool for Saturday and Sunday at the user's location.

Give one short line per day: conditions and the high and low. Then say which day is better for being outside, and when rain is expected if any.

## Don't

- Don't list hourly details.
- Don't answer for today only.
- Don't give severe weather alerts.
