## Why this works

**It is opinionated.** Apple's own guidance uses a files app as the example: a skill is your chance to say what "clean up" means in your app. Without it, the model invents its own definition.

**It looks before it acts.** Getting the whole folder first, then confirming a plan, keeps a bulk change from surprising anyone.
